# Before you start

This is a practice interview. Please read this and accept to continue.

- **How you are assessed.** An AI interviewer talks with you and scores your answers and your code. Your interviewer, not you, receives the scores and feedback.
- **Your voice.** Your speech is converted to text as you talk. **No audio is recorded or stored.**
- **Your resume.** The resume you applied with is used to choose questions. Your contact details are removed before anything is processed.
- **Anonymised answers are processed by OpenAI.** Only text with your contact details removed is sent to OpenAI's AI models, and it never includes your name beyond your first name. OpenAI may use this text to improve its models.
- **What we keep.** A redacted transcript and your results are kept for **90 days**, then deleted automatically. You can ask for them to be deleted sooner.
- **Full screen.** The interview runs in full screen in Chrome. **Leaving full screen, switching tabs or closing the window ends the interview**, and what you did up to that point is still scored. Hold Esc to exit.
- **Coding.** While you write code, the voice is paused and the microphone is off.

By continuing you agree to the above.
